# RESQ — Rescue Food. Save Money. Waste Less.

## What is RESQ?

RESQ is a mobile-first food rescue platform designed to connect customers with local businesses that have safe, good-quality surplus food.

Instead of allowing unsold food to become waste, RESQ gives vendors a simple way to offer surplus items at discounted prices while giving customers an affordable way to discover and rescue them.

## The Problem

Every day, bakeries, cafés, restaurants, and grocery stores can have food left over near the end of their operating hours.

This creates a simple but important mismatch:

* Vendors have surplus food.
* Customers want affordable food.
* Good food can become waste simply because it is not discovered in time.

RESQ focuses on making surplus food visible, accessible, and easy to collect.

## How RESQ Works

The core experience is intentionally simple:

**Surplus food → Discounted listing → Local discovery → Reservation → Pickup → Impact**

A vendor can list available surplus food with its quantity, discounted price, and pickup window.

Customers can browse available rescue opportunities, compare savings, open a listing, reserve it, and receive a pickup code.

## Key Features

### Live Food Rescue Feed

Users can discover available surplus food through a simple mobile-first feed.

### Discounted Rescue Bags

Listings clearly display the original price, rescue price, percentage discount, quantity, and pickup window.

### Simple Reservation Flow

Users can move from discovery to reservation and checkout without unnecessary steps.

### Pickup Code

After confirmation, the customer receives a pickup code for the rescue.

### Orders

Users can view active and completed food rescues in one place.

### Impact Tracking

RESQ presents the user's contribution through metrics such as money saved, food diverted, and estimated environmental impact.

## RESQ Plus

RESQ also introduces a premium experience for users who want faster access to new surplus drops.

Premium concepts include:

* First Dibs
* Smart Drop Alerts
* Advanced Filters
* Smart Auto-Reserve
* Advanced Impact Analytics
* Rescue streaks

The free experience remains focused on making food rescue accessible, while premium features focus on convenience, discovery, and early access.

## Why This Approach?

The goal is not simply to build another food marketplace.

RESQ focuses specifically on the time-sensitive nature of surplus food.

A food item that is available today may have little value tomorrow. Therefore, visibility, timing, location, and a fast reservation experience are central to the product.

**Customers:** affordable food and meaningful savings.

**Vendors:** an opportunity to recover value from surplus inventory.

**Environment:** less potentially usable food entering the waste stream.
